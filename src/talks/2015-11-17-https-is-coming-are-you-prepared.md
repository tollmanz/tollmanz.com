description: "Google, Firefox, and the IETF are currently engaged in significant initiatives to convert the Web to be secure by default. According to SSL Pulse, 75% of the top 1 million websites that use HTTPS are not actually secure because of misconfiguration. In my talk, I will discuss the key aspects of HTTPS to empower developers to deploy truly secure HTTPS sites."
